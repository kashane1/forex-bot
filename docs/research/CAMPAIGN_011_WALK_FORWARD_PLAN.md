## 8. Limitations of this plan

- **Plan is structural, not evidence.** The plan validates fold
  geometry only. No backtest has run; no metric exists.
- **In-fold no-leakage is not enforced by the harness.** The
  candidate's no-lookahead guarantees (seed input never reads
  bar-`t` price data; only `close[t]` is consulted for stop
  placement; ATR comes from index `-2`) are the defence;
  the harness only enforces between-fold non-overlap.
- **Date-only fold edges.** The harness uses `datetime.date`
  units; intraday H4 bar alignment is handled by the bespoke
  engine.
- **Frozen mode only.** Per-fold-from-train /
  -from-validation modes are unauthorized. The runner will
  refuse anything other than `parameter_mode == "frozen"`.
- **Identical to CAMPAIGN_010 by design.** The plan is a
  byte-for-byte copy of CAMPAIGN_010's plan with the campaign
  label changed. This is deliberate — the entry-signal
  comparison is on identical fold geometry.

## 9. Why this plan does not approve anything

- It contains no strategy parameter values (those live in the
  YAML and the strategy module).
- It runs no backtest; it produces no `FoldMetrics`.
- It produces no expectancy R, no profit factor, no per-pair
  number, no aggregate.
- `WalkForwardPlan.strategy_evidence` is `false` by
  Pydantic-enforced invariant — flipping it raises at
  construction time.
- **CAMPAIGN_011 cannot be approved by design** — even a
  passing `WalkForwardResults.overall_verdict` (unexpected
  under random entry) would trigger the investigation playbook
  per
  [`CAMPAIGN_011_PRECOMMIT_CHECKLIST.md`](CAMPAIGN_011_PRECOMMIT_CHECKLIST.md)
  §12, never promotion.
