## 6. Limitations of this plan

- **Plan is structural, not evidence.** The plan validates fold
  geometry only. No backtest has run; no metric exists. Per
  [`WALK_FORWARD_HARNESS_STATUS.md`](WALK_FORWARD_HARNESS_STATUS.md)
  §3 the harness explicitly does not execute backtests.
- **In-fold no-leakage is not enforced by the harness.** The
  candidate's no-lookahead guarantees (close-only at bar `t`, ATR
  from `t-1`, structural source-grep tests) are the defence; the
  harness only enforces between-fold non-overlap.
- **Date-only fold edges.** The harness uses `datetime.date`
  units; intraday H4 bar alignment is handled by the bespoke
  engine (which already does this).
- **Frozen mode only.** Per-fold-from-train / -from-validation
  are unauthorized. The runner will refuse anything other than
  `parameter_mode == "frozen"`.

## 7. Why this plan does not approve anything

- It contains no strategy parameter values (those live in the
  YAML and the strategy module).
- It runs no backtest; it produces no `FoldMetrics`.
- It produces no expectancy R, no profit factor, no per-pair
  number, no aggregate.
- `WalkForwardPlan.strategy_evidence` is `false` by
  Pydantic-enforced invariant — flipping it raises at
  construction time.

A `WalkForwardResults` cannot exist for this plan until the
campaign code (Phase 3) drives `BacktestEngine` against each
fold's test window and writes `FoldMetrics`. Approval requires
both that result AND items 4–6 of the six-evidence ladder per
[`NEXT_RESEARCH_DIRECTION_AFTER_CAMPAIGN_002_REJECT.md`](NEXT_RESEARCH_DIRECTION_AFTER_CAMPAIGN_002_REJECT.md)
§8.
