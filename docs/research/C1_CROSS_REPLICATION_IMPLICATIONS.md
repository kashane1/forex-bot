## A. Failure branch (the realized case)

### A.1 Should C1 be retired?

**Yes — as a discovery target, retire it. Keep it on the shelf as a control.**

- The C1 **trading lane** was already CLOSED on the majors (cost-defeated;
  `FAIL_FRONT_GATE`). The remaining open scientific question was whether its
  *effect* was real beyond the USD majors. This sprint answered it: **no.** The
  effect that was statistically significant on EUR_USD/USD_JPY **does not
  replicate** on non-collinear crosses — it is best explained as specific to the
  USD-major discovery pairs / USD-regime structure (`C1_ARTIFACT`).
- With both the **tradability** question (closed: cost) and the **generality**
  question (closed: does not replicate) resolved negative, C1 has **no remaining
  open lane.** Continuing to probe C1 — new thresholds, new vol cuts, new pairs —
  would be the re-tune the whole programme forbids.
- **Retire C1 as an active research target.** Retain the locked definition,
  runner, and artifacts as a **null/control reference** (a known, well-characterized
  non-replicating effect is a useful yardstick for future factors). Reopen *only*
  under the standing universal condition: **new data** (≥10–15y history, true
  tick/L2, a lower-cost venue) or a **fundamentally new external thesis** — and
  then only via a fresh pre-registered screen, never a re-fit of C1.

### A.2 Does S2 become the preferred direction?

**Yes.** The planning sprint pre-committed exactly this contingency: if C1
evaporates as an artifact, "the next move is the **financing-data prerequisite**
… or … more importantly **S2 — the cross-implied currency-strength index**."
Reasoning, now that the contingency is realized:

- The C1 failure is **informative for S2, not discouraging.** C1 was a
  *single-instrument, USD-discovered* confluence effect; its non-replication is
  evidence that **USD-major-specific structure does not generalize** — which is
  precisely the *motivation* for S2, a **breadth-pure, currency-level** factor
  that removes USD-collinearity by construction rather than inheriting it.
- S2 does **not** depend on C1 being real; it is the highest-ranked *new* family
  (Phase-4 rank 4.17, second only to C1's now-spent replication), needs **no new
  data**, and tests a genuinely different hypothesis (cross-sectional currency
  strength) on the same populated universe.
- **Carry (S-carry / F12–F14) remains prerequisite-blocked** on real swap-rate
  ingest and is *not* promoted by this verdict; it stays a future *data* sprint.

So the preferred next discovery direction is **S2 (currency-strength index)**,
with the financing-data ingest as the alternative enabler if/when carry is
prioritized. Phase 7 writes that prompt.

### A.3 Broader implication for the programme

This is the **second** independent line of evidence (after the pair-space-sign
analysis on the majors) that the programme's "edges" are entangled with
**USD-regime structure** rather than universal market mechanics. The disciplined
takeaway: **breadth-pure, cross-sectional constructions (S2/S3) are a better bet
than single-instrument directional confluence**, because they attack the
USD-collinearity that both C016/C031 *and* now C1 reveal as the confound. The
cross data delivered exactly the value it was justified on — **independent
replication** — and returned a clean, decision-relevant negative.

## B. Success branch (NOT realized — recorded for completeness)

Had the verdict been `REPLICATION_SUCCESS`, the implications would have been:

- **What it would have meant:** C1 is a genuine, universe-independent multi-TF
  confluence-reversion factor — real beyond the USD majors.
- **What it would NOT have meant:** that C1 is **tradable.** Replication ≠
  tradability. C1 is already cost-defeated on the *cheaper* majors; cross spreads
  are *wider* and fatter-tailed, so a genuine effect would still face a *higher*
  cost wall. A successful replication would have warranted only a **separate,
  later, pre-committed** cost/front-gate evaluation (Stage 3) — **never** an
  automatic campaign or approval.
- **Why replication ≠ tradability (the standing distinction):** replication asks
  *"is the effect present and null-separated?"* (gross, descriptive). Tradability
  asks *"does it survive realistic round-trip spread + financing at a stress
  multiple?"* (net, executable). C1 has always passed the first kind of question
  on its discovery pairs and failed the second; the cross test only ever
  addressed the first, and on the crosses it now fails even that.

This branch is moot under the realized `REPLICATION_FAILED` verdict.

## C. One-line takeaway

C1 does not replicate outside the USD majors → its significant magnitude was a
**USD-regime artifact**; **retire C1 as a research target** (keep as control) and
make **S2 (cross-implied currency-strength index)** the next discovery direction —
no campaign, no strategy, no approval, freeze intact.
