## 4 · Reconciling with Phase 2 (concentration)

The classifier reports `pair_concentration = 30.21%`. The Phase 2
[concentration diagnostic](CAMPAIGN_015_CONCENTRATION_DIAGNOSTICS.md)
reports USD_CHF = **54.5% of total R**. These are not in
contradiction — they measure different things:

- The classifier's `pair_concentration` is *gross positive R per pair*
  / *total gross positive R*. USD_CHF has many large winners
  *and* many full-stop losers, so its share of the winning side is
  more modest (30.2%).
- Phase 2's 54.5% is *net total R per pair* / *aggregate total R*. It
  captures who is carrying the aggregate edge after losers are
  netted out.

Both views are honest. The classifier's gate is the binding
pre-committed one, and it passes. The Phase 2 view is an informational
warning: the **net edge is unusually USD_CHF-dependent**, and any
follow-up candidate should probably treat single-pair leverage as a
first-class design question rather than as something to discover post-run.

The Phase 2 top-5-trades = 77% concentration is *not* one of the
classifier's gates, but it is the next concern: a strategy that
depends on 5 outlier trades over 4 years × 7 pairs is statistically
fragile in a way that a per-fold t-stat does not directly measure.

## 5 · Reconciling with the runner REJECT verdict

The runner's pre-committed *gating* set rejects the campaign on two
aggregate gates: `fold_pass_rate_ge_5_of_8` (0/8) and
`trade_count_min_200` (164 < 200). Both ultimately trace to the
per-fold `trade_count_ge_30` gate, which every single fold fails
(max 28 trades per fold).

The anti-overfit classifier deliberately does **not** include a
trade-count gate. It tests *whether the per-trade edge is real, given
the trades that did happen*. So:

- **Conditional-on-the-trades-that-fired**, the edge is robust above
  the matched random-entry null with high statistical significance.
- **Unconditionally**, the campaign is too sparse to clear its own
  pre-committed gates, and one fold is negative.

A genuinely promising sparse edge is the kind of thing that
*deserves* a follow-up: more data, more pairs, a lower-frequency
confirmation. Phase 5 will weigh whether that follow-up is justified
or whether the Phase 2 concentration concerns warrant a STOP instead.
