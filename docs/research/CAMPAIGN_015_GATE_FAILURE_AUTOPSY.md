## 9 · Are positive results broad or concentrated?

By the gate-failure autopsy alone — i.e., before the dedicated
concentration analysis in Phase 2 — the picture is mixed:

- **Broad signals**: 7 / 8 folds non-negative on expectancy; 6 / 7
  pairs aggregate-positive; single-pair dominance only 30.2% at the
  aggregate level. Median per-fold expectancy R = +0.2588, which is
  *close* to the mean (+0.2300) — i.e., not pulled hard by one
  outlier fold.
- **Concentration signals**: extreme cell-level sparsity (38% of
  cells have ≤ 1 trade); 1 of 8 folds is negative; only fold 1 (with
  77% single-pair dominance) suggests pair concentration locally.
- **The headline profit-factor of 107.55 is itself a sparsity
  artifact**: with only 164 trades and 1 of 8 folds negative on
  expectancy, a denominator of "gross losing R" near 0 is what
  pushes PF to three digits. The Phase 2 concentration analysis
  needs to look at top-trade and top-cell contribution to confirm
  this.

Provisional verdict for this phase: **the failure mode is sparsity,
not single-fold concentration**. Phase 2 will quantify whether
sparsity additionally implies trade-level or pair-level
concentration.
